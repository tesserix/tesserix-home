# Regional vending research and hospital approval

Reviewed 18 October 2026. This is a research and operating checklist, not a food
licence, clinical recommendation or certification. The questionnaire measures stated
interest; it does not establish demand, regulatory permission or patient suitability.

## Bhubaneswar first, adaptable elsewhere

Share these anonymous links:

- https://questionnaire.tesserix.app/vending-customers?market=bhubaneswar
- https://questionnaire.tesserix.app/vending-business?market=bhubaneswar

They prefill India, Odisha, Bhubaneswar, INR and the non-personal campaign code
`bhubaneswar-pilot`. Everything remains editable. Add a campaign parameter for a
specific venue/distribution round; it overrides the preset campaign. Do not put
personal information in a link. The generic URLs work for all supported countries.

Keep the core questions comparable: venue, actual visit times/frequency, hypothetical
purchase frequency (including never/unsure), up to five product families, first choice,
optional item details, dietary preferences, flavour/temperature preferences, stated
maximum snack/drink price, payment and reasons not to buy. The business form asks for
position-specific footfall, decision authority, opening hours, existing competition,
product requests, rent/revenue share, electricity, internet, servicing, security,
maintenance, waste and approvals. Estimates/unknown remain valid answers.

The initial Odisha options include mudhi mix, chakuli/poda pitha, chhena poda, khaja,
dahi bara aloo dum and bel pana alongside tea/coffee, juices, shakes, yoghurt, fruit,
nuts, bars and packaged snacks. These are **candidate concepts**: neither traditional
recipes nor popularity elsewhere prove they can be packaged safely or sold profitably
from a machine. Odisha Tourism documents the regional cuisine, including puffed rice,
pitha, chhena poda and dahi bara. It supplies cultural context, not customer research:
[official cuisine guide](https://odishatourism.gov.in/en/cuisine-culinary-trails).

Indian state suggestions use the venue's state, including common state-code aliases.
Examples include khakhra/dhokla in Gujarat, vada pav/poha in Maharashtra, filter coffee
and idli in southern states, mishti doi in West Bengal and sattu snacks in Bihar and
nearby states. These are editorial hypotheses, not claims about every resident.
Respondents can explore all cuisines and give free-text brands/pack sizes. Unknown
states retain generic Indian and global options; they never prevent a response.

International examples include flat whites, matcha, kopi/teh tarik, karak chai,
calamansi juice, laban, onigiri, baguettes and oat bars. All 20 currently supported
countries retain global choices and local currency. Do not infer religion, ethnicity,
diet or purchasing power from country. Ask dietary preferences directly. UPI/QR is
labelled for India; elsewhere the option is local QR. No exchange rate or purchasing
power adjustment is applied to prices. Exact SKU nutrition and storage requirements
must be checked independently, including products marketed as protein or low sugar.

## Validate behaviour before purchasing

1. Pilot the questions with a small mix of gym members, shoppers, venue staff and
   hospital staff/visitors. Ask whether any wording, food names or price units confuse
   them. The forms are currently English; they are not an Odia/Hindi translation.
2. Collect across weekdays/weekends and morning/evening shifts. Record public venue
   and campaign codes; avoid surveying only friends, one gym class or one employer.
3. Segment by country, state/region, venue and campaign in console. Region matching
   ignores case/outer spaces but is exact text, not geocoding or alias normalization.
   Check spelling and separate Bhubaneswar from other Odisha locations with campaigns.
4. Compare category votes and first choices, item-level counts and price sample sizes.
   New detail questions are optional; missing older details are not negative votes.
   Category wording now includes snack/protein bars; note this change when comparing
   older category totals. Never pool different currencies or compare unsegmented totals.
5. The console's 30-response/30%/10% thresholds are exploratory planning rules, not
   statistical significance or a representative sample guarantee. Questionnaire
   submissions do not verify unique humans. Report selection/distribution bias.
6. Run a small approved stock pilot. Measure actual sales, margin, stock-outs, expiry,
   waste, servicing costs and refunds before replenishment. Pricing research alone
   cannot determine order quantities or profitability.

## Hospitals: three separate gates

**Food law** governs the operator/product/process and location. **Hospital policy**
controls procurement, nutrition, infection control, placement and access. **Clinical
diet approval** controls patient-specific food and therapeutic nutrition. Passing one
gate does not pass the others. A visitor buying a snack is a different use case from
providing a meal to a patient under a prescribed diet.

| Product/process                                                                        | Initial decision                                 | What must be checked                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sealed water, labelled shelf-stable packaged snacks                                    | Candidate for staff/visitor retail               | Approved supplier, labelling/allergens, nutrition classification, expiry, traceability and hospital sign-off                                               |
| Tea/coffee, hot water                                                                  | Hold until equipment/process approval            | Water quality, cleaning, electrical/fire/accessibility and burn controls, caffeine rules, servicing                                                        |
| Yoghurt/lassi, fresh juice, refrigerated shakes, cut fruit, sandwiches                 | Hold until validated food-safety plan            | Exact manufacturer's storage instructions, cold chain from supplier to sale, shelf life, monitoring, power-loss lockout and safe disposal                  |
| UHT/aseptic sealed drinks/shakes                                                       | Assess exact SKU                                 | Some are shelf-stable unopened; dairy content alone does not establish refrigeration requirements                                                          |
| Dahi bara, pitha, other prepared dishes                                                | Concept research only pending process validation | Licensed preparation/packing, safe cooling/holding/reheating where applicable, very short shelf life, cleaning and suitable vending mechanics              |
| Sugary drinks, salty/fatty snacks, energy drinks, nuts, glass packs                    | Policy review/hold                               | Jurisdiction-specific nutrition thresholds, allergens, caffeine/child access and hospital restrictions; no universal category-wide hospital ban is assumed |
| Unsafe/recalled food, untraceable supply or required labelling missing                 | Do not stock                                     | Quarantine, recall and disposal procedures; applicable expiry/use-by limits                                                                                |
| Prescribed diets, clinical formula, medicines or supplements making therapeutic claims | Outside this retail pilot                        | Separate clinical, pharmacy and regulatory pathway; the survey does not authorize them                                                                     |
| Alcohol, tobacco and other age-restricted goods                                        | Outside this pilot                               | Not part of the food retail scope; do not interpret omission as a statement about every jurisdiction's law                                                 |

Do not describe any SKU as safe for all patients, diabetic-friendly or allergy-safe
without the applicable clinical and regulatory basis. No patient identity, diagnosis,
ward/bed information or medical history is requested. An optional dietary preference
is not a diagnosis or a clinical order.

## Site approval record before a hospital pilot

Obtain written ownership/sign-off from facilities, procurement/food services, a
hospital dietitian and infection control as applicable. Record the exact location,
audience, permitted SKUs, nutrient/allergen criteria, supplier evidence, food-business
approval, insurance, cleaning/refill responsibility, service hours, continuous power,
monitoring/alerts, unsafe-temperature or power-failure lockout, emergency contact,
recall traceability, expiry handling, accessibility, refunds and waste arrangements.
Keep evidence in the approved operational system; do not request private access codes
in a public form. Survey approval checkboxes are self-reported leads for follow-up,
not a signed approval record. Missing approval means hold the pilot.

The public landing page links the hospital summary. The business form shows extra
placement/audience/policy/approval questions only for hospital venues; server validation
rejects hospital-only answers on other venue types. The customer form remains general
retail research. This application does not implement machine temperature monitoring,
SKU approval enforcement, clinical diets or automatic purchasing.
